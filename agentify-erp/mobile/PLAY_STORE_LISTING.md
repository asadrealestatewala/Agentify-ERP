## Full description (max 4000)
Agentify ERP puts your real estate or construction business in your pocket. Sign in to your workspace to manage leads, properties, clients, projects, staff and finances from anywhere.

What you can do
• Leads and clients: capture, follow up and convert leads, keep notes, tasks and call history in one place.
• Properties and visits: browse listings, see them on the map, check in to property visits and navigate to them.
• Field team: when your company enables it, agents can share their location while working, with a clear consent notice. Location is used in the foreground only.
• Meetings and calls: join video meetings and record calls using your camera and microphone.
• Documents and photos: upload files and take photos straight into records.
• Finance and operations: invoices, payments, expenses, reports and approvals.
• Construction: projects, tasks and site progress for your teams.
• Your workspace, your branding, your region: works with multiple countries, currencies and languages.

Good to know
• You need an Agentify ERP workspace account. Plans and billing are managed on our website.
• Camera, microphone and location are requested only when you use a feature that needs them.
• You can request account closure inside the app (Plan & Billing) or at agentifyerp.co/delete-account.

Questions? Visit agentifyerp.co.

## Review risks to prepare for
WebView-only apps may be rejected under the Minimum Functionality policy; stress native camera, microphone, GPS and Back handling, and consider enabling push notifications (Firebase). Keep subscription purchase on the website only, with no links to it inside the app.
