# Content Calendar posting through Zernio (v1.70)

Code: `server/src/services/zernio.js` (the only code that calls Zernio), `socialCalendar.js` (stages, approval, caps, the due-post tick, Retry), `zernioAdmin.js` (console), `routes/content.js`, public webhook `POST /api/platform/webhooks/zernio` in `routes/platform.js`. UI: `pages/Content.jsx`, `components/ConnectedAccounts.jsx`, console `pages/platform/SocialPosting.jsx`. Test: `server/test/zernio.test.js` (simulated Zernio).

## Rules
* Stages: draft, pending_approval, approved, scheduled, posted, failed (`content_posts.stage`). Old posts: posted stays, ready becomes approved, the rest draft. Old `status` is still kept in step for the manual flow.
* Only a user with Content > approve can approve or send back. Self-approval is allowed; `approved_by` and `approved_at` are kept. Editing words, media or accounts of an approved post returns it to pending_approval; moving the date or time does not.
* We hold the schedule. A one-minute tick sends due `scheduled` posts to Zernio with `publishNow` and an `x-request-id` (`erp-t<tenant>-p<post>-a<attempt>`), then reads Zernio's answer (`GET /posts/{id}` and the webhook). Webhook payloads are never trusted for state; they only say which post to re-read.
* Caps: per platform account per rolling 24 hours (`zernio_caps`, Instagram 25 to start; Zernio's page says 100), plus the plan's `posts_per_day` / `posts_per_month` once Part 3 adds them. A capped post stays `scheduled` with `blocked_reason`.
* Failure keeps the reason (`fail_reason`); Retry sends only accounts that did not succeed. An unreachable Zernio is retried by the tick for 6 hours before the post is marked failed.
* Rollout: off for every workspace until listed in Console > Social posting (`zernio_workspaces`). Unlisted workspaces keep the manual "tick it off" flow.

## Billing modes (`zernio_mode`)
* `platform`: one Agentify key, one Zernio profile per workspace (`externalId` `tenant-<id>`). Zernio bills the Agentify team per connected account.
* `workspace`: the workspace saves its own key (encrypted in its settings; only the last 4 are ever returned) and Zernio bills it directly.

## Secrets
Keys are stored encrypted (`platformConfig` for the platform key, `zernio_key_enc` per workspace) and never returned, logged, or put in error messages. The webhook secret is generated by us when the webhook is registered and checked as HMAC-SHA256 hex over the raw body (`X-Zernio-Signature`).
